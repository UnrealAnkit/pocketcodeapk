# Taste Learnings

- Prefers not to have assumptions made on their behalf — when a decision point arises, they want to be asked directly rather than having the assistant proceed with a guess. Confidence: 0.95
- Values thoroughness and completeness; explicitly dislikes cutting corners, leaving slack, or overlooking edge cases. Confidence: 0.90
- Encourages independent research — providing documentation links and instructing the assistant to consult docs and search the web on its own rather than relying solely on what is already known. Confidence: 0.85
- Prefers pure Expo/TypeScript solutions — explicitly wants to migrate everything to TS/React Native/Expo and avoid Kotlin or other native modules, even when a native module is already written and small. Confidence: 0.90
